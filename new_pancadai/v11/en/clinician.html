Integration

Four weeks, contract to go-live

A dedicated project manager throughout; radiologist and technologist training included.

Week 1 · Assessment

Review equipment (CT model, PACS version) and workflow; define report format and alert rules.

2

Week 2 · Integration

AI server setup and PACS/RIS interfacing; automatic DICOM routing; zero extra steps for radiology.

3

Week 3 · Validation & training

Validation on institutional data plus training for radiologists and technologists (online course included).

Go-live, pilot run, performance dashboard delivered. Monthly quality reports thereafter.
