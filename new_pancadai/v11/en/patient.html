Walk through his day↓
01
06:00 · Dawn

A beautiful day to be out

Since retiring, he rides along the riverbank every morning. Bright sun, gentle breeze — his favorite kind of day. He felt fine. Early pancreatic cancer is quiet like that.

02
08:30 · Routine

One coffee, one newspaper

He never imagined a routine checkup would change everything. His wife booked a full health screening last week — “Just a check. For peace of mind.”

03
10:30 · The Scan

Ten minutes in the CT room

He lay still for ten minutes. The scan finished and images were uploaded for reading. He dressed and headed home — unaware that an AI was carefully reading his images.

04
11:10 · AI Reading

1.2cm — AI saw it

Deep in the images, AI circled a 1.2cm mark — 92.1% sensitivity under 2cm. Drag the divider to see what AI found.

◂ Drag to compare ▸

05
02:00 PM · The Doctor

“Found early. No need to panic.”

A radiologist reviewed AI's findings, and his specialist explained: “The tumor is still very small. We have plenty of time to discuss the options together.”

06
04:30 PM · Options

Found early means more options

Drag the slider to compare five-year survival between “found early” and “found after metastasis” — this is why every millimeter matters.

Found early (<2cm) Found after metastasis

* Survival figures are public statistics (Japanese Pancreatic Cancer Registry; SEER ~3% for metastatic), for education only. Actual outcomes depend on individual condition and treatment.

07
07:00 PM · Dinner

The whole family at the table

Because it was found early, options remained. He sat at the head of the table while his grandson ran laps around it. His wife said: “We'll face this together.”

08
09:30 PM · Starlight

Living well is the best medicine

On the balcony at night, he looked at the stars. Treatment hadn't started, but the fear was already gone — because every step, someone was watching over him.
