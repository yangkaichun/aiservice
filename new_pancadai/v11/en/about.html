About PanCAD.ai

Making early detection reachable.

We are PanCAD.ai — a Taiwan startup born from NTU research, focused on one thing: pushing the moment of pancreatic cancer detection earlier.

Our Mission

Every CT deserves a second look

Pancreatic cancer's five-year survival is under 10%, mostly because it's found too late. We believe if AI can help radiologists see tumors while they're still small, thousands of families would have different stories.

Patient first

Every product decision starts with “does it help patients?” — not a tech demo, but a guardian of life.

Evidence first

Every claim is backed by literature and regulatory approval. Honest data is our basic respect for medical partners.

Walking with physicians

AI doesn't replace doctors; it lets them see earlier and more precisely. We stand with radiology and GI teams.

Prof. Wei-Chung Wang

Professor, Institute of Applied Mathematical Sciences, NTU | Founder & Director, MeDA Lab | Co-founder, PanCAD.ai

Ph.D. in Applied Mathematics, University of Maryland (1996). Over 25 years in medical AI and high-performance scientific computing, with 100+ publications (Nature Medicine, The Lancet Digital Health, Radiology). Rigorous mathematics that makes AI judgment trustworthy.

🔬 Smart Healthcare & Technical Innovation

  • Breakthrough Device Designation: led the team that developed PANCREASaver — the AI pancreatic cancer detection system — receiving FDA Breakthrough Device Designation in 2022 and the Taiwan MOHW medical device license in 2023.
  • Exceptional clinical precision: the system accurately predicts whether a CT scan contains pancreatic cancer and its location — 92.1% sensitivity for tumors under 2 cm, catching up to 92% of early lesions easily missed by the naked eye or radiologists.
  • Cross-domain industrialization: co-founded PanCAD.ai to turn laboratory research into commercially viable, clinically practical medical software — elevating Taiwan's international competitiveness in smart healthcare.

優化醫療工作條件

計畫痛點：醫護過勞、重複性閱片工作繁重。

PanCAD 解方：作為放射科醫師的 AI 影像 Copilot，自動預篩影像，提高檢驗效率並降低死亡率。

導入智慧科技醫療

PanCAD 解方：源自台大研究的成熟 AI 檢測產品，無縫介接醫院 PACS 系統，滿足深耕計畫技術規格。
