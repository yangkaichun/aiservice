The product

An AI second reader that never blinks

PANCREASaver® is FDA Breakthrough-designated medical device software (SaMD). The moment an abdominal CT scan finishes, it reads the images, marks suspicious pancreatic lesions, and sends the alert back into the radiology workflow — so early pancreatic cancer is no longer missed.

Fully automated, real-time reading

Analysis starts automatically when the scan completes — no extra steps for physicians. CNN deep learning and radiomics engines run in parallel.

Small tumors, still detected

92.1% sensitivity for lesions under 2cm — closing the gap where eyes and conventional tools miss.

Clinically validated

5 international journal papers and a nationwide multicenter AUC of 0.95, led by the National Taiwan University Hospital team.
