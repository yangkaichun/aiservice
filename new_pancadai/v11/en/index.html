Why early detection matters

Pancreatic cancer can be difficult to detect early.

Tumor stage and other clinical factors help guide treatment planning. PANCREASaver® is designed to support review of suspicious findings on abdominal CT.

13% Overall five-year relative survival is about 13% overall and about 44% for localized disease (U.S. SEER data). Earlier detection can expand treatment options; individual outcomes vary.
<2cm The cited study reported 92.1% sensitivity for tumors under 2 cm in its defined study population.
Found late = few options By the time symptoms appear, the tumor has often spread. AI-assisted review of routine CT studies is designed to find the lesion before it becomes a late-stage diagnosis. Why screening matters ↗

Figures as published in peer-reviewed literature and PanCAD.ai’s clinical validation. See the evidence center for sources.

Questions about AI-assisted pancreatic CT review

These answers summarize published evidence and current product scope. They are for education; discuss personal decisions with a clinician.

Early pancreatic cancer may cause few or nonspecific symptoms, and small lesions can be difficult to detect on abdominal CT. Screening and imaging decisions should follow clinician guidance.

A CNN + radiomics dual-engine analyzes contrast-enhanced abdominal CT images and presents suspicious findings for radiologist review; PACS/RIS alerts depend on the institution’s integration setup.

Five-year relative survival is about 13% overall and about 44% for localized disease (American Cancer Society and NCI SEER data). Earlier detection can expand treatment options; individual outcomes vary.

People with a family history or a known hereditary syndrome should discuss risk assessment with a specialist. Age, smoking, or diabetes alone does not mean routine pancreatic cancer screening is appropriate; asymptomatic average-risk adults should not undergo routine screening.
