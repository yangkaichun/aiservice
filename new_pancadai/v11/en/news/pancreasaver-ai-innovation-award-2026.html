2026 AI INNOVATION AWARD

From 435 Proposals to Three Winning Teams: PANCREASaver® Earns 2026 AI Innovation Award

Recognizing collaboration with National Taiwan University and National Taiwan University Hospital in AI-assisted pancreatic cancer detection. Join the team in Taipei on December 3.

PanCAD.ai · Published 2026.11.09

Proposals
435
Winning teams
3

PanCAD.ai has won the 2026 AI Innovation Award for PANCREASaver®, as one of three winning teams selected from 435 proposals. Organized by Taiwan’s National Science and Technology Council, this year’s award focuses on smart healthcare. The recognition marks a milestone in the team’s work on AI-assisted pancreatic cancer detection.

Bringing clinical insight and AI together

PanCAD.ai extends its sincere thanks to the teams at National Taiwan University (NTU) and National Taiwan University Hospital (NTUH) for their collaboration. PANCREASaver® uses AI image analysis to assist physicians in identifying suspected pancreatic cancer lesions on computed tomography (CT) images, providing an additional tool to support early detection.

The award highlights the value of collaboration across research, medicine and industry. Building on this recognition, the team will continue developing medical AI around the needs of clinical practice.

Join us in Taipei on December 3

On December 3, 2026, PanCAD.ai will take part in the award ceremony and innovation dialogue at the InnoZone Stage, 4F, Taipei Nangang Exhibition Center, Hall 1. Healthcare professionals, industry partners, startups and researchers are warmly invited to celebrate this milestone and exchange ideas on bringing AI innovation into medical settings.
