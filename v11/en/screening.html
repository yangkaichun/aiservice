Why pancreatic AI

Why add pancreatic AI screening to your health check?

Pancreatic cancer has one of the lowest survival rates — and almost no early symptoms. Your clients need it; nobody offers it yet.

Real demand

Pancreatic cancer ranks among Taiwan's top-10 cancer killers; the high-risk population (60+, family history, diabetes, smoking) keeps growing.

A true differentiator

Beyond routine heart-lung checkups, AI pancreatic screening makes your center the place that “sees earlier.”

Proven technology

TFDA license No. 007946, FDA Breakthrough, AUC 0.95 — regulatory and literature-backed, ready to convince.
